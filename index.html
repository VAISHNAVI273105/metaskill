The Photographer

"Photography is not about documenting reality, but revealing the emotional pulse that resides beneath it."

I am Julian Vance, founder and principal artist at Lens & Light Studio. With over a decade behind manual prime lenses, my work is rooted in natural light, authentic cadence, and an uncompromising editorial standard.

Every commission is treated as a bespoke gallery exhibition — timeless, unfiltered, and deeply evocative.
