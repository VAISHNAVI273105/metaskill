Clarity & Details

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you travel for destination weddings and international campaigns?

Yes. Approximately 60% of our annual commissions take place overseas — from Lake Como and the Scottish Highlands to Tokyo and Kyoto. Travel and lodging are quoted as flat, transparent line items with zero hidden surcharges.

How many photographs do we receive?

For full-day weddings, clients typically receive between 500 and 700 curated, fully-edited images. For portrait and fashion sessions, between 40 and 60 final retouched frames are delivered. We prioritize depth and emotional weight over endless repetitive filler.

What is your delivery timeframe?

A "First Glimpse" curated gallery of 25–30 images is delivered within 48 hours following your event. The complete archival gallery and physical fine art album are delivered within 4 to 6 weeks.

How do we secure our date on your calendar?

A signed commission agreement and a 30% retainer secure your date. Because we accept a strictly limited number of commissions each season, reservations are accepted on a first-come, first-served basis.
